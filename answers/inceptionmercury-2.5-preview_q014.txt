

The passage implies a formative relationship rather than a purely diagnostic one. The command to "lay up" suggests agency: by choosing where to invest, we direct our inner loyalty. It is not just that wealth exposes existing affection; it is that investment cultivates it.

Possession requires vigilance. What we hold demands our focus, drawing our attention inward or upward. This sustained focus roots our love in that object. Earthly stores decay, anchoring devotion to the temporary. Eternal stores endure, aligning desire with the enduring. Ultimately, the passage binds what we keep to where we belong, showing that priority dictates devotion.
