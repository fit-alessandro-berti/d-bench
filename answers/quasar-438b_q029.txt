A posture of **non-retaliatory, humble correction** is required: the spiritually mature person is to *restore* the faulted brother, but to do so in **meekness** (humility/gentleness), while also **suspecting himself** — looking to his own motive and temptation.

They are kept together because the verse wants correction to be **restorative rather than punitive**:

- **Restoration** names the aim: bring the man back toward right standing.
- **Meekness** names the manner: do it gently, not with pride or vengeance.
- **Self-suspicion** names the guard: the spiritual man must examine himself, because the very fault he sees in another might tempt him too.

So the moral logic is: *help the erring man, but help him humbly and with self-knowledge*.
