

The passage defines affection by contrasting it with common human flaws. By listing what it is not, the text clarifies that genuine care requires actively suppressing ego-driven impulses like jealousy or arrogance. These exclusions highlight that true connection demands self-mastery rather than mere emotional inclination.

The vision of fortitude presented here is internal and resilient. Rather than dominating others, this capacity is shown through restraint, patience, and the willingness to prioritize others' needs. It is the ability to remain steady amidst provocation and to sustain hope despite hardship. This redefines might as the power to endure and protect, rather than to control or assert superiority.
