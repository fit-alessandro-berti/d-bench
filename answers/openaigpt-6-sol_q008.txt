Love for God is invisible as a claim, but it becomes visible in how a person treats others. In 1 John, loving a “brother”—especially a fellow believer—is not an optional extra to loving God; it is part of obeying the God who first loved us. If someone refuses to love a person right in front of them, their claim to love the unseen God rings false.

The self-deception is mistaking religious feeling or profession for love while making an exception for the real person one resents. The verse does not say that loving people earns God’s love. It says that professed love for God cannot be genuine while hatred is being protected and justified.
