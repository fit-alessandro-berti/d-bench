The danger is **the craving to be seen, not visibility itself**. The key phrase is “to be seen of them”: Jesus asks what the giver wants the act to accomplish. Someone may witness a gift meant to help a neighbor. The moral danger comes when the giver uses the neighbor’s need as an occasion to win admiration.

This distinction also explains why Matthew 5:16 can speak approvingly of others seeing good works: there, the aim is that they glorify the Father. Here, the giver wants the praise. The gift may still help its recipient, but the giver has sought a different reward. A useful test is: *Would I still give if no one knew the gift came from me?*
