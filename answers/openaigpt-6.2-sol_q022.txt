The psalmist asks to be searched because being defended is not the same as being right, and being comforted is not the same as being changed. Even if others have wronged him, he still wants to know whether there is something in his own heart that needs correcting.

Earlier in the psalm, God’s knowledge of him is already a given. So “Search me” is not a request for God to discover something new; it is a willingness to face what God sees. The honesty here is neither self-defense nor self-condemnation. The psalmist does not insist on his innocence or assume the worst about himself. He asks *if* there is a wicked way in him, and then asks to be led. The point of being searched is not to dwell on his faults, but to be shown a better way to live.
