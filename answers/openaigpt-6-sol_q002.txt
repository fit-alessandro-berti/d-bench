Pardon addresses what David has done; a clean heart addresses what has gone wrong *within* him. He does need forgiveness, but he knows that relief from guilt alone would leave him capable of returning to the same sin. “Create in me” is a plea for God to make him different, not merely to let him feel better.

The rest of the prayer shows what he wants that change for: to remain in God’s presence, to recover the joy of salvation, and to be sustained by a willing spirit. Wanting only escape from guilt asks, “Can this be over?” Wanting a clean heart asks, “Can I become someone who loves what is right?” Psalm 51 holds both needs together: mercy for the past and renewal for the life ahead.
