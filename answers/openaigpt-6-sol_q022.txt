To ask only for defense or comfort might leave one’s own part of the problem untouched. The psalmist asks God to *search* him because he wants more than vindication: he wants to know whether anything in his own heart or conduct needs to change.

The honesty modeled here is humble without being despairing. It admits, “I may not see myself clearly,” and makes room for unwelcome truth. But the request ends with “lead me”—the purpose of being searched is not merely to feel guilty; it is to be guided toward the way everlasting.
