The problem is **not visibility itself**, but **the craving to be seen and praised by others**.

In Matthew 6:1, the key phrase is “to be seen of them.” Jesus is warning against doing acts of righteousness with the wrong motive: performing for human applause rather than seeking to honor God. The danger is not that someone might see a good deed, but that the doer’s heart is seeking **self-glory, reputation, or approval from people** instead of love for God and neighbor.

Visibility becomes morally dangerous when it becomes **performance for an audience**, because then the “goodness” is being used as a tool for self-promotion. That motive, Jesus says, forfeits the true reward from God.
