The danger is **the desire “to be seen” and admired**, not visibility itself. Public generosity becomes morally distorted when winning human praise is its purpose: the giver’s attention shifts from helping others and honoring God to displaying personal virtue.

Jesus is not forbidding every good deed done in public. In Matthew 5:16, he tells his followers to let others see their good works so that they glorify God. The difference is the **aim**: directing attention toward God and the good being done, rather than seeking applause for oneself.
