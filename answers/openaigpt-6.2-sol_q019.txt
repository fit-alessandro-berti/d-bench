James is exposing a contradiction between **honoring God and dishonoring people made in God’s image**. The same mouth blesses the Father and curses someone who bears his likeness. James’s point is not merely that our tone changes from one moment to the next. It is that our contempt for another person contradicts the reverence we profess toward God.

Speech reveals this disorder quickly because words are often our first response when we are angry, offended, or inconvenienced. We may sincerely bless God in worship, yet a sharp reply can expose how readily we set aside another person’s dignity when they cross us. A careless word does not tell the whole story of someone’s heart, but it can show where what we confess has yet to shape how we treat others.
