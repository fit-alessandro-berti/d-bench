It says **both — and the key is that the text makes the connection almost circular.**

Jesus says:

> “For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.”

The sentence moves from **treasure → heart**. The heart follows the treasure. That sounds more like **treasure trains the heart** than merely reveals it. But the verse also assumes that what you *choose to treasure* reveals what you already love. So both are true:

- **Your treasure reveals your heart** — if you look at what you store up, protect, and value, you can see where your heart actually is.
- **Your treasure trains your heart** — what you deliberately store up and invest in will pull your heart toward it. The heart becomes attached to the treasure.

In the context, Jesus is commanding action: *Lay up treasure in heaven, not on earth.* The reason is: if you store your treasure on earth, your heart will end up there. Therefore, **deliberately place your treasure in heaven** — give, serve, love, seek the kingdom — and your heart will be drawn there too.

### Possession, attention, and love are tied together like this:

- **Possession**: What you “treasure” is what you hold as valuable, secure, and yours. It is the thing you possess or want to possess.
- **Attention**: Where your treasure is, your eyes, thoughts, worries, and plans keep going. You watch it, maintain it, worry about losing it.
- **Love**: The heart in the Bible is not just feelings; it is the center of your desires, thoughts, and will. When your attention is fixed on something, your desire attaches to it, and love follows.

So the “heart” becomes tied to the “treasure” by **possession → attention → love**. That is why Jesus warns against earthly treasure: an earthly treasure is fragile and fading, so a heart tied to it is fragile and fading too. But a heavenly treasure is secure and eternal, so a heart tied to it is secure and eternal.

In short:  
**Treasure reveals what is in the heart, but it also re-locates the heart. That is why Jesus says to choose where you lay up treasure wisely.**
