**External compliance** means doing what the law requires while one’s desires and loyalties remain unchanged. A **“new heart”** means an inward change: the person becomes willing and able to respond to God, so obedience grows from within rather than being only imposed from outside. Verse 27 connects that change to God’s Spirit, who “cause[s] you to walk” in God’s ways.

The image of a **stony heart** makes hardness sound like a material condition because Ezekiel is describing more than an occasional bad choice. Stone is unresponsive; a “heart of flesh” is living and responsive. The point is that hardened resistance needs not merely better instructions but replacement through God’s action. Here, *flesh* is the positive image—not weakness or sin, but a heart capable of responding.
