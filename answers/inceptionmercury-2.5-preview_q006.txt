

In this context, the "self" being denied typically refers to the ego-centric will or personal autonomy placed above spiritual commitments. It involves setting aside selfish ambitions and the desire to control one's own path independently of divine guidance.

For many, this is liberating because it relieves the burden of carrying life's weight alone. Shifting focus from self-preservation to a larger mission can provide profound purpose and reduce anxiety rooted in personal security.

Conversely, this message can offend those who prioritize individualism and self-actualization. The call to sacrifice personal interests may seem like a demand for submission or a rejection of human dignity. In cultures that champion personal rights, the idea of losing one's life to gain it can appear contradictory or unnecessarily restrictive.
