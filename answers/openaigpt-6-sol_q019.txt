James exposes the contradiction of **praising God while treating people made in God’s likeness with contempt**. The same mouth claims to honor God and dishonors those who bear his image. “These things ought not so to be” because love of God and regard for people cannot be separated so easily.

Speech reveals moral disorder quickly because it turns our inward attitudes into outward action—often before we have time to make ourselves sound better. Blessing may show what we profess; a curse spoken in anger can show how we actually regard someone. James is not forbidding honest correction. He is warning against a heart that worships God yet feels free to demean people.
