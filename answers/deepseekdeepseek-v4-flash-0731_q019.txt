The inner contradiction is this: **we use the same mouth to bless God as Father and then curse people who are made in God’s image.**

Since humans bear God’s likeness (Genesis 1:26–27), to curse them is to dishonor God Himself. It is like blessing a king while spitting on his portrait. The person is saying, in effect, “I honor God,” while cursing the very thing that reflects God. So the contradiction is not in God—it is in the human heart.

Why does speech reveal moral disorder so quickly? Because **words come from the heart**. Jesus said, “Out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks” (Matthew 12:34). The tongue exposes what is inside us more quickly than almost anything else. James says the tongue is “a fire” and “a world of iniquity” (James 3:6). It can bless and curse from the same mouth because the heart is divided—professing love for God while holding contempt for neighbor.

James compares this to a fountain bringing forth both sweet and bitter water, or a fig tree yielding olives. It is unnatural—it reveals a disordered soul. Speech exposes moral corruption instantly because it is the overflow of what we truly are.
