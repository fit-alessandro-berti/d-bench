Jesus pictures the heart as a **treasury**: what a person stores and cultivates inwardly—good or evil—eventually comes out in speech. “Out of the abundance of the heart” means words are not detached from character; they draw on what fills it.

So the passage suggests **all three**:

- **Symptoms:** speech can show the moral condition beneath it.
- **Choices:** people still choose what to say and are responsible for it; Jesus goes on to speak of giving account for words (vv. 36–37).
- **Revelations:** especially in a pattern of speech, what comes out makes hidden loyalties and attitudes visible.

Jesus’ point is not that every stray word perfectly defines a person, or that someone can never conceal what is in their heart. It is that speech is morally significant because it both **comes from** the heart and **discloses** what has been stored there.
