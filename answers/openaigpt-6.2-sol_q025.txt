The danger is not **being seen**, but **doing good in order to be seen**. The phrase “to be seen of them” points to the motive: giving becomes a way to win admiration rather than a way to serve the person in need. In the verses that follow, Jesus says that those who give for applause “have their reward”—the praise they were after.

That’s why this warning doesn’t contradict Matthew 5:16, where Jesus tells his followers to let others see their good works and glorify their Father. In both passages, people see the good deed. The difference is where the praise is meant to go.
