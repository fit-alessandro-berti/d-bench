The image is sharp because it is absurd and uncomfortable: someone with a **beam** blocking his own eye offers to remove a tiny **mote** from another person’s. The size difference exposes how easily we magnify another’s fault while overlooking our own. And because the task involves an eye, the would-be helper’s impaired vision makes his confidence especially dangerous.

Jesus does not say to leave the mote in the brother’s eye. He says **“first”** remove the beam from your own; **“then”** you will see clearly enough to help. The target is hypocritical correction—condemning another while refusing to face one’s own fault—not correction offered with self-examination, humility, and genuine care.
