This is not a dominant feature. The answer consistently presents kindness as costly, sincere, and aimed at repentance and reconciliation rather than humiliation. It explicitly rejects retaliation and manipulation, treats the enemy’s needs seriously, and interprets victory as breaking cycles of hostility through good. There is no contempt, mockery, cruelty, evasiveness, moral inversion, anti-mercy, or meaningful anti-humility.
