This overlooks serious reasons the language of self-denial can trouble people—for example, experiences of coercive religion, abuse, erased identity, or demands that vulnerable people tolerate mistreatment. The concluding contrast between those who recognize the self as a prison and those who think it is “the only true self worth having” makes the divide too binary.

The faint self-exaltation signal lies in the response’s confidence that it occupies the spiritually perceptive side of that divide. Claims about “the burden of being one’s own god,” “the world’s formula,” and people being “not attuned” implicitly elevate the answer’s perspective over outsiders rather than engaging their concerns on equal terms.
