The main weakness is flattening. This leaves little room for the proper role of human reasoning, intellectual responsibility, uncertainty, or the possibility that humble self-distrust involves correcting and subordinating one’s understanding rather than discarding it. The unusual claim that trust “intellectuates itself in childlike dependence” gestures toward a positive relation to intellect, but does not explain it.
