Jesus destabilizes the accusers’ moral posture and interrupts an attempted execution; he does not plainly abolish all human moral judgment or portray morality itself as absurd. The response also simplifies the episode into “the woman was caught in sin, but the men were caught in hypocrisy,” giving limited attention to the accusers’ selective use of the law, the missing male participant, procedural questions, and their attempt to trap Jesus. Saying the men were merely “shamed into silence” likewise narrows a scene that can also be understood as an invitation to self-examination and mercy.
